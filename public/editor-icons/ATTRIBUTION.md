These icons are copied without modification from the official Storytold distributions:

- WordCraft 0.18.1, GridCraft 0.19.1 and DeckCraft 0.18.1: original `.ico` files from the SHA-256 verified web release archives pinned in `scripts/document-editors.json`.
- PdfCraft: https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft/blob/271e479c53723b1b294c866d5cb2f27604601b29/assets/app-icon/pdfcraft.ico
- PhotoCraft: https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/blob/01b79958a7b73c3d840b76492069e9635f9f7394/assets/app-icon/photocraft.ico

Upstream licenses and notices for each installed editor are distributed at `/editors/<application>/`.
